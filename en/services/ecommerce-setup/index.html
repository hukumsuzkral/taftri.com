Service 03

Your own store, ready for order one.

Selling on marketplaces is a start; your own store keeps the margin, the customer data and the brand with you. We handle the setup end to end — platform choice, payment and shipping integrations, e-invoicing and measurement. When we hand it over, the store can take its first order.

Scope

What this
covers.

Platform Choice & Setup Shopify, Ikas, WooCommerce or Ticimax — we pick what fits your model and build it. Theme configuration, domain connection and collection architecture included.
Product & Catalog Migration Bulk product import, variant and stock structure, category architecture and description templates.
Payment & Shipping Integration Payment gateway, installment tables, cash on delivery; carrier integration, delivery rules and return flow.
ERP, E-Invoicing & Accounting Automating order, stock and invoice flows; marketplace-to-store inventory sync.
Measurement Setup GA4, Meta Pixel, Google Ads tag, Conversions API and e-commerce event tracking — so you can see what advertising actually does.
Legal Pages & Privacy Distance selling agreement, cancellation and return terms, cookie and privacy notices.
Speed & Mobile Checks Core Web Vitals measurement, mobile experience testing and a pre-launch test order.
Launch & Team Training Training on the admin panel, order management and content updates. You end up able to run the store yourself.
How we work

A four-step
roadmap.

  1. 01 / 04

    Needs Analysis

    We map product count, variant structure, shipping and payment needs. The platform decision is made here.

  2. 02 / 04

    Setup & Design

    The store is built, the theme shaped to your brand, and the catalog architecture created.

  3. 03 / 04

    Integration & Migration

    Payment, shipping, ERP and e-invoicing connections are built; products are migrated.

  4. 04 / 04

    Testing & Launch

    Test order, speed and mobile checks, measurement verification, then launch and team training.
