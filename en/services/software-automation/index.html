How we work

A four-step
roadmap.

  1. 01 / 04

    Process Discovery

    We measure how much time each task actually consumes and rule out the ones automation would never pay back.

  2. 02 / 04

    Solution Design

    Which systems connect, how the data flows and where the scope ends is agreed before any code is written.

  3. 03 / 04

    Development & Testing

    The software is built and tested against real data. We verify the output together before it goes live.

  4. 04 / 04

    Rollout & Monitoring

    It goes live and your team is trained. Error alerting and monitoring are set up so you know it keeps working.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions.

Tools like Zapier and Make are good for standard flows, and we recommend them first. But subscription cost climbs quickly with volume, complex rule sets cannot be expressed in them, and debugging gets hard. Custom software makes sense once you hit those three limits — we tell you at discovery which one you are at.

We measure the hours saved during process discovery. A process that consumes 10 hours of manual work a week typically covers its build cost within a few months. When the payback looks long, we advise against building it — and show you that calculation up front.
